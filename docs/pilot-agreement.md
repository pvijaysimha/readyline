# ReadyLine Pilot Agreement (draft)

> Plain-language starting draft, not legal advice. Have a Washington business attorney review it before charging anyone.

This agreement is between **ReadyLine** ("we") and **{{CLIENT_BUSINESS_NAME}}** ("you"), starting **{{START_DATE}}**.

1. **What we provide.** For 14 days, we set up and run an automated phone assistant that answers calls forwarded from your business line when you don't pick up, collects caller details, books estimates or promises callbacks, and texts you a summary of each call.
2. **Cost.** The pilot is free. After 14 days, the service continues only if you agree in writing to the standard price of {{MONTHLY_PRICE}} per month, plus a {{SETUP_FEE}} setup fee. There is no automatic charge.
3. **Your responsibilities.** You set up call forwarding, give us accurate information about your services, prices and service area, and respond to the leads we send you.
4. **Call recording and disclosure.** Calls are recorded. The assistant tells every caller at the start that the call is recorded and that it is an automated assistant. You agree not to remove these notices.
5. **Your data.** Caller information and recordings belong to you. We use them only to run and improve your service, never sell them, and delete them within {{RETENTION_DAYS}} days of the end of service unless you ask us to send them to you first.
6. **Texts.** We send texts only to people who contacted your business and only about their request. Anyone who replies STOP is never texted again.
7. **No guarantee of results.** We can't guarantee how many calls, bookings or jobs you'll get. The assistant may occasionally misunderstand a caller; you remain responsible for confirming jobs and prices with your customers.
8. **Limits.** Our total responsibility under this agreement is limited to the amount you paid us in the previous {{LIABILITY_MONTHS}} months (during the free pilot, $0).
9. **Ending.** Either of us can end the pilot or service at any time by text or email. You then turn off call forwarding, and we shut down your assistant within 2 business days.
10. **Third-party tools.** The service runs on third-party phone and AI platforms. If a platform has an outage, we'll tell you and restore service as soon as we can.

{{CLIENT_NAME}}, {{CLIENT_BUSINESS_NAME}} — Signature and date: ______________

{{YOUR_NAME}}, ReadyLine — Signature and date: ______________
