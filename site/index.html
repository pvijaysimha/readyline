How it works

Three steps, no new app

1

You miss a call

Your business line forwards unanswered calls to ReadyLine. You keep your number, and you still answer whenever you can.

2

3

A summary lands on your phone seconds after the call. Leaks and other urgent calls get transferred straight to your cell.

Straight answers

Questions owners ask

Will callers know it's not me?

Yes. It says up front that it's your automated assistant and that the call is recorded, which Washington law requires.

Can a caller still reach me?

Always. Anyone who asks for you gets a promised callback time, and urgent calls are transferred to your cell.

Do I need a new number?

No. You keep your number and turn on forwarding for unanswered calls, which takes about five minutes.

Who owns the call recordings?

You do. We use them only to run your service and delete them when you leave, unless you want a copy.

I already use Jobber or Housecall Pro.

Those include their own phone assistants. Try theirs first. ReadyLine is for owners who don't use one and want it set up and managed for them.

Will it quote prices?

Only the ranges you approve. Final prices always come from you after you see the job.
